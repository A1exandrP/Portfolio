Profile

Twenty years in infrastructure operations have taught me one thing: the difference between a fragile environment and a reliable one is almost never about the tools. It is about the standards, the automation, and the discipline to treat infrastructure as a product rather than a collection of one-off fixes.

Most of what I build is designed to outlast me. At B2BSoft, the gold image framework I put together became foundational to how the team deployed servers long after I left — and proved its value during a production incident before anyone realised it would need to. At REW, the documentation standards I established are still being applied across new migration projects years later.

I have delivered 50+ modernization programs for Microsoft 365, Azure, SharePoint, and endpoint platforms. I reduced MTBF from ~80h to ~3,000h and MTTR from ~3h to ~30min — not through heroics, but through consistent automation and standardization.
